## The entry

> A queue is a human caterpillar, each person a segment shuffling forward behind the one in front.
>
> It maps how the line moves as one slow body and stops when its head stops, but it leaves out that every segment has its own mind and somewhere else to be.

Line 1 explains a queue in a single vivid sentence built on one analogy a
child can picture: a caterpillar. Line 2 says in one sentence why that
analogy works — what it maps (the line moving and stopping as one slow body)
and what it leaves out (every person has their own mind and somewhere else
to be). The entry is 252 characters, inside the 1–400 character gate.
